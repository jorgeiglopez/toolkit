# Rules
- Only the Organization JSON-LD schema stays inline in SKILL.md as the pattern example; Article, Product, FAQ, and Breadcrumbs schemas live in `references/structured-data.md`.
- Page experience ranking factor (Core Web Vitals) is out of scope here, always link to `core-web-vitals` instead of restating LCP/INP/CLS guidance.
- Backlinks/authority and most of content quality are explicitly out of scope, don't invent guidance for them.
- Checklist items are grouped by priority (Critical/High/Medium/Ongoing), not by SEO sub-area, keep that grouping when updating it.
